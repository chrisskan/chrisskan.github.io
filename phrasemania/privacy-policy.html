Phrasemania – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Phrasemania (in Greek: Η Φρασοκατάληξη; "the game", "we") handles on Android and iOS. The game is published by NWBN. Contact: chris96skan@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Player names and settings (players, time per turn, rules, lives, language, sound and vibration) are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, we do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The game does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or precise location.

2. Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device model and operating system, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information. Google processes this under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you (App Tracking Transparency). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

3. In-app purchase

A one-time purchase removes all ads. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details. The store only tells the game whether the purchase was made, so it can be restored.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not store your data ourselves, please use the privacy settings above or Google's controls. For any question, email chris96skan@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
