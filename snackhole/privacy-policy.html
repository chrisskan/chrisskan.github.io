Snackhole – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Snackhole ("the game", "we") handles on Android and iOS. The game is published by NWBN. Contact: chris96skan@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level, coins, unlocked items, boosters, league and Battle Pass progress, and settings (volume, vibration) are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, AdMob and the ad networks it works with (AppLovin, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Liftoff Monetize, Mintegral and Pangle) may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. Each provider processes this under its own privacy policy:

  • Google: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Liftoff: liftoff.io/privacy-policy
  • Mintegral: mintegral.com/en/privacy
  • Pangle: pangleglobal.com/privacy

Your choices. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. Buying No ads removes the ad breaks between matches; ads you choose to watch for a reward remain optional.

2a. Measuring our TikTok ads

On Android the game uses the TikTok App Events SDK so we can measure the ads we run on TikTok. It sends TikTok the install and app opens, when you complete a level, and when you buy something (product and price), together with device information and the advertising ID. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland this only happens if you allow it in the consent form shown at first launch (and you can change that in Settings → Privacy settings). TikTok's privacy policy: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game which items you own so they can be unlocked and restored.

4. Leaderboards

League leaderboards are simulated inside the game. Other names on the leaderboard are generated by the game; no personal data is shared with other players.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for teens.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not store your data ourselves, please use the privacy settings above or contact the ad providers listed. For any question, email chris96skan@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
